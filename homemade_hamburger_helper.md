# Homemade Hamburger Helper

A one-pot riff on the [NYT recipe](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1020728-homemade-hamburger-helper) — bacon left out, and the hot sauce swapped for flavor without the spice.

## INGREDIENTS
* 4 tablespoons neutral oil (canola or vegetable)
* 1 large yellow onion, diced into ½-inch pieces
* 1 teaspoons salt (plus more to taste)
* 0.5 teaspoons black pepper
* 3 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 pounds ground beef
* 1 cups dry white wine
* 3 cups chicken stock or water
* 0.8 cups heavy cream
* 2 tablespoons tomato paste
* 2 tablespoons apple cider or white wine vinegar
* 1 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
* 2 teaspoons sweet smoked paprika
* 1 bay leaf
* 8 ounces elbow pasta
* 5 American cheese slices, ripped into small pieces
* 1.5 cups grated Cheddar (smoked cheddar for extra depth)
* 0.5 cups finely chopped chives

## STEPS
1. Caramelize the onion: Heat 4 tablespoons neutral oil (canola or vegetable) in a large (12-inch) sauté pan or Dutch oven over medium-low. Add 1 large yellow onion, diced into ½-inch pieces and season lightly with 1 teaspoons salt (plus more to taste) and 0.5 teaspoons black pepper — go easy, since the sauce builds flavor later. Cook until the onions turn light beige and begin to caramelize.
2. Add garlic: Add 3 garlic cloves, minced and cook until fragrant and just starting to brown.
3. Brown the beef: Raise heat to medium-high and add 1 pounds ground beef, breaking it up with the back of a spoon. Cook until the liquid has mostly evaporated and the meat starts to sear and form a crust on the bottom of the pan.
4. Drain the fat: Off the heat, carefully drain most of the fat — but leave a little behind to keep the meat moist and rich (there's no bacon fat here, so don't take it all).
5. Reduce the wine: Return to medium-high heat, add 1 cups dry white wine, and let it reduce until the mixture is almost dry.
6. Build the sauce: Stir in 3 cups chicken stock or water, 0.8 cups heavy cream, 2 tablespoons tomato paste, 2 tablespoons apple cider or white wine vinegar, 1 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce, 2 teaspoons sweet smoked paprika, and 1 bay leaf. Mix until combined and bring to a boil.
7. Cook the pasta: Once boiling, add 8 ounces elbow pasta and cook until al dente, stirring often.
8. Melt the cheese: Reduce heat to low and stir in 5 American cheese slices, ripped into small pieces and 1.5 cups grated Cheddar (smoked cheddar for extra depth), stirring until fully melted and the sauce thickens.
9. Finish and serve: Off the heat, stir in 0.5 cups finely chopped chives and season to taste with salt and pepper. Serve immediately.

## NOTES
Hot-sauce swap: the tomato paste, vinegar, and Worcestershire together replace the tang, body, and savoriness of the hot sauce without any heat. For a one-ingredient version instead, purée a jarred roasted red pepper with a splash of vinegar and stir that in at Step 6. Bacon note: sweet smoked paprika + Worcestershire cover the smoke and umami it was providing; a pinch of extra salt at the end rounds it out.
