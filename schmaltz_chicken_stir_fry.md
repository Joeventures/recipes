## Ingredients

* 2 tbsp schmaltz (rendered chicken fat)
* 1 sweet onion, sliced
* 2 carrots, julienned
* 2 celery stalks, slicked
* 2 boneless chicken breasts, thinly sliced
* 2 tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tsp honey
* 1/2 tsp ground ginger
* 1 clove garlic, minced
* Cooked rice or noodles

## Directions

1. Heat schmaltz in a pan over high heat. Add chicken and stir fry until golden. Remove from pan.
2. Add onions, carrots, and celery. Stir-fry until softened.
3. Return chicken to the pan. Add soy sauce, honey, and ginger. Stir well.
4. Cook for another 3-5 minutes until everything is well coated.
5. Serve over rice or noodles.

**Note:** Stores like Sprouts and Whole Foods used to carry schmaltz from Epic Provisions, but not so much anymore. Epic stopped making it, sadly.
Some Kroger locations with a fully-stocked kosher section offer small containers of it from Empire Kosher.
Amazon offers jars of it from brands like Cornhusker Kitchen and Fatworks.
The bad news is that there aren't a lot of options. The good news is that the few options that are out there are all good ones.
