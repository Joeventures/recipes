# Sweet Tea Brined Roast Chicken
Originally from [this NYT recipe](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1025338-sweet-tea-brined-roast-chicken), but modified to be more flavorful and less spicy.

## Ingredients
* 1 gallon-ish sweet tea
* Juice from 1 lemon
* 1 tbsp minced garlic
* 1/4 cup Dash (a.k.a. Mrs. Dash Original)
* 1/4 cup Montreal Steak Seasoning
* 1 tbsp paprika
* 2 chicken leg quarters
* Neutral-flavored oil for greasing the pan
* 1/2 tsp kosher salt
* 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Instructions
1. To a large pot, add sweet tea, lemon juice, garlic, Dash, Montreal Steak Seasoning, and paprika. Whisk to dissolve.
2. Submerge chicken in the brine, cover, and refrigerate for 24 hours.
3. When ready to cook, heat the oven to 425 degrees, and grease a rimmed baking sheet with oil or nonstick cooking spray. Remove the chicken from the brine and pat dry with paper towels.
4. Place chicken skin-side up on the baking sheet and sprinkle with salt and pepper. Roast until the chicken is cooked to an internal temp of 165 degrees (35 minutes). If you find the skin is getting too dark, cover with aluminum foil.
